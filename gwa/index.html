    How this is worked out
    • A subject's final grade is the mean of its grading periods, rounded to a whole number. While periods are still blank, the figure shown is a running average of what you've entered.
    • The general average is the mean of those rounded subject finals, shown to two decimals.
    • Passing is 75. Honours bands are 90–94, 95–97 and 98–100, and every subject must also be at least 85.
    • The planner doesn't use algebra — it tries every whole grade from 0 to 100, recalculates the whole thing each time, and reports the lowest one that actually reaches your target. That way the rounding is handled exactly rather than approximately.

    Thresholds live in one place at the top of js/app.js. Check them against your school's current guidelines before relying on this for anything that matters.

    Built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript — no libraries. Your grades are saved in this browser only and never leave your device.
